Types of Intellectual Property

Understanding different forms of IP protection

Patents

20 years

Protect your inventions and technical innovations

Examples: New products, processes, machines, compositions

Trademarks

10 years (renewable)

Protect your brand identity and business name

Examples: Logos, brand names, slogans, designs

Copyrights

Life + 50 years

Protect original creative and artistic works

Examples: Software, books, music, artistic works

Trade Secrets

Indefinite

Protect confidential business information

Examples: Formulas, processes, customer lists
